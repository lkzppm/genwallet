# GenWallet

A genetic algorithm that builds a portfolio of 10 Ibovespa companies and operates it over 10 real B3 sessions (2026-10-27 to 2026-11-10). Every order is committed to this repository before the open it targets, and every decision can be replayed from its archived inputs and seed.

UFRJ, Inteligência Artificial: *"Criar Carteira de Ações com 10 empresas e operar geneticamente durante 10 pregões"*.

The design lives in [`spec/`](spec/README.md).

Paper money only. Not investment advice.
